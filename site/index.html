About Jose

The voice behind it.

Jose teaches entrepreneurs on a daily group call. The lessons here come from those calls, from 2024 on. Each course follows one book, in the book's order. Each lesson is his reading of it: what he agrees with, what he adds, his stories and his questions.

These are his own analyses, not summaries of the books, and they are education, not advice.
